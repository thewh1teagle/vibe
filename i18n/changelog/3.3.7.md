## New

- ⬇️ **Background update downloads** — a new version is now downloaded quietly while you work and waits until you want it. Installing is one click: Vibe updates, restarts by itself and comes back where you left it, with no second question. Nothing is ever installed or restarted unless you ask. You can turn the downloads off in `Settings → General`
- 🔔 **Update reminder** — an update that has been waiting for two days shows a small bar inside the app with Later and Restart to update. No pop-ups and no system notifications

## Fixed

- 🫥 **Starting hidden** — when Vibe was set to launch at startup, its window could still appear and take focus. It now stays in the tray until you open it
