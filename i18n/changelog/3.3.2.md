## New

- 📝 **Sentences and paragraphs** — a new Lines switch in the transcript's View options rebuilds the transcript as one sentence per line or as paragraphs, so a long recording reads the same way from the first minute to the last. A new paragraph starts when the speaker changes or after a long pause. It works while transcribing and on finished transcripts, editing still works in every view, and Original is always one click away
- 📄 **Exports that read like the screen** — text, Markdown, HTML, Word and PDF exports follow the Lines view, so a transcript read as paragraphs is saved as paragraphs. Subtitles (SRT, VTT) keep their short lines
- 🪜 **Every step before the first line** — while a transcription gets ready it now lists all the steps (preparing the audio, recognizing speakers, starting transcription) and marks the one it is on, so you can see what is still to come

## Improved

- 🚀 **Long files start much sooner** — the wait before the first line of a long recording is several times shorter. A one-hour file that took about 30 seconds to start now takes about 6 in our test
- ⏱️ **Every word keeps its time** — new transcripts save the timing of each word, which is what lets Sentences and Paragraphs be rebuilt at any time without transcribing again. Transcripts made before this version can still be regrouped, a little less precisely

## Fixed

- 🎯 **Word timestamps with Whisper** — after the first line, word times were adjusted against the wrong part of the audio. They now follow the audio they belong to
